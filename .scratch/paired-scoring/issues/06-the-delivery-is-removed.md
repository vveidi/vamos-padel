# 06: The delivery is removed

**What to build:** nothing — this ticket is closed unbuilt.

**Blocked by:** 05

**Status:** wontfix

It was written when the paired match was to replace both ways of scoring alone.
It does not: pairing is a third way, chosen by a setting on the device the match
is started on (ADR-0017), and a match scored on the watch alone still needs the
delivery, the receipt, the queue, the `delivered` column and the watch's store
to reach the history. ADR-0002 and ADR-0004 stand.

The glossary half of it — **Paired match**, **Remote**, **Intent**, **Live
link**, and **Scorer** rewritten rather than removed — landed with the triage
that closed it.
